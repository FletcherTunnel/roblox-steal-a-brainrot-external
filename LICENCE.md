## 🧩 About

**Roblox Brainrot Game** is a collection of Brainrot-themed games on Roblox. It includes Steal a Brainrot, Dig a Brainrot, Zoom for Brainrots, and Rizz Lucky Block RNG .

**Steal a Brainrot** is a multiplayer tycoon game where players buy, steal, and defend Brainrot characters — voxel characters based on Italian brainrot memes [citation:1][citation:4]. It is one of the most popular games on Roblox, with over 25 million concurrent users [citation:4][citation:10].

**Dig a Brainrot** is a digging game where players dig graves for items and monsters, inspired by Steal a Brainrot [citation:3].

**Zoom for Brainrots** is a racing game where players drive through chaotic maps and spam brainrot sounds [citation:13].

**Rizz Lucky Block RNG** is a RNG simulator where players roll the machine to collect rare Brainrots and generate passive income [citation:9].
